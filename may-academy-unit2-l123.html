💬 Sentence Patterns

I've got a brother.I have got a brother.
I haven't got a sister.I have not got a sister.
Who's this?This is my cousin.
Who vs WhatWho → people · What → things
Contraction: Who is → Who's. Khi nói về người dùng Who; khi hỏi về đồ vật dùng What.
