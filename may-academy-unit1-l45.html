💬 Sentence Patterns

I've got ...
→
I have got a book.
I've = I have
I haven't got ...
→
I haven't got a pen.
haven't = have not
What's this?
→
It's a pen.
What's = What is · It's = It is
Positive / Negative
✓ / ✕
I've got a window.
I haven't got a poster.
